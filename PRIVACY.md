# Duo Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app **Duo Games: 1 2 3 4 Players**, developed by Tianyang Wang.

## Game data on your device

Player names, animal choices, settings, scores, saved ad-removal access and free-play availability are handled on your device. The app does not require a game account, and we do not operate a server that receives your player profiles or game scores. Device and cloud backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

## Optional advertising

Some Random Five entries and additional game swaps offer rewarded ads through Google AdMob. The app requests non-personalized advertising and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing: Google's advertising SDK may collect device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, ad and app interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate its services. Google processes this information under its own policies. Consent and privacy-choice forms are shown when required by Google's consent system.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). When available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app to review your choices.

## Purchases

Apple handles payments for the optional, one-time No Ads purchase. The app uses Apple's verified transaction information to provide and restore access. We do not receive your card number or bank-account details. You can manage purchases and request eligible refunds through Apple.

## Sharing and support

The share button opens Apple's system share sheet with the public App Store link. It does not upload your player names or scores. If you contact us through public support issues, the content you submit is handled by GitHub and may be visible to others. Please do not post passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local preferences and gameplay data remain until you remove the app's data through your device settings. Apple transaction records and Google advertising data follow the respective provider's retention rules. Contact those providers for requests relating to the data they control. Removing the app does not cancel or erase an Apple purchase record used for restoration.

## Children and families

Duo Games is a general-audience shared-screen game. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of optional advertising and purchases and may use Apple's device-level restrictions.

## Contact and changes

For questions about the app or this policy, visit our [support page](SUPPORT.md). We may update this policy when the app's practices change; the date above identifies the current version.
