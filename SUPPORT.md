# Duo Games Support

13 quick games for one to four players on one iPhone or iPad.

## Playing

Choose the number of players at the bottom of the home screen. Tap a player to choose an animal or edit their name, then choose a game. In one-player mode, a robot joins automatically. The question-mark button explains the current game's rules and any skill pickups. Pause to change settings or return home.

## No Ads and restore purchases

No Ads is an optional one-time Apple in-app purchase. Use Restore Purchases in the home Settings menu when signed in to the Apple account used for the purchase. A crown indicates verified ad-removal access. Payment and refund requests are managed by Apple.

## Ads and Random Five

The first two Random Five entries are free, followed by a free entry every six hours. Extra entries and additional swaps may require a completed rewarded ad. Ads require an internet connection and may be temporarily unavailable. Every individual game can also be selected from the home screen.

## Report a problem

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/asssaver97/duo-games-support/issues/new) with your device model, iOS version, app version, game name and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords, bank details or personal player names in a public report. You may also use Apple's Report a Problem service for purchase-related issues.
