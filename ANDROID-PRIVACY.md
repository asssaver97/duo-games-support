# Duo Games Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy describes the Android app Duo Games: 1 2 3 4 Players, developed by Tianyang Wang.

## Game data

Player names, animal choices, settings, scores and free-play availability are stored on the device. No game account is required. We do not operate a server that receives player profiles or game scores. Android and device-provider backups may retain app data according to your device settings.

## Optional rewarded advertising

Some Random Five entries and additional game replacements offer rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Individual games can be played without choosing these rewarded advertisements. We request non-personalized ads. Google's advertising SDK may process device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, ad or app interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate its services. We do not provide your chosen player names, avatars or game scores to the ad request.

We do not request precise-location permission. Approximate location may be inferred from an IP address. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing.

Google processes advertising information under its own policies. Required consent forms are provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. Where required, Settings → Privacy choices lets you review or withdraw your choices. An ad is requested only when the consent system permits it.

See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Purchases

This Android build does not currently offer in-app purchases or collect payment details. Apple purchases do not create an Android purchase entitlement.

## Retention and deletion

You can remove local profiles, settings and free-play data by clearing this app's data or uninstalling it through Android settings. Device backups and Google advertising records follow the respective provider's settings and retention rules. Contact those providers for requests concerning records they control.

## General audience

Duo Games is a general-audience shared-screen game. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of optional advertising and use device-level restrictions where appropriate.

## Support and changes

For questions, visit the [Duo Games support page](https://github.com/asssaver97/duo-games-support). Public GitHub support issues may be visible to others; do not post passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. We may update this policy as the app changes; the date above identifies this version.
